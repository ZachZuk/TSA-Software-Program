About This Tool

What is this tool?

This is a tool made for farmers as well as ordinary people. Providing a picture of a leaf from the plant as well as the plant type, our AI detects what disease it has with 98-100% accuracy. It also gives people the treatments to the disease, helping them keep more plants healthy.

Who made it?

This tool was made by two highschool students, Zachary Zukosky and Aaravjeet Singh, as a project for a club.

Why was it made?

We wanted to make something that could help increase agricultural sustainability and efficency. A tool to reduce crop loss using AI was a great way to do this.
