About This Tool

What is this tool?

This is a tool made for farmers as well as ordinary people. Providing a picture of a leaf from the plant as well as the plant type, our AI detects what disease it has with 98-100% accuracy.

Who made it?

This website was developed by our talented in-house web development team, combining modern design principles with user-friendly functionality.

Why was it made?

Our goal was to create an accessible online presence that effectively communicates our brand message while providing value to our visitors. We wanted to build a platform that makes it simple for customers to find what they're looking for.
