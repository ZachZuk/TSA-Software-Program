How to Use

How do I use it?

To diagnose a plant, you first need to get to the diagnose page, which can be accessed through the navbar or from the home page. Then, you need to select the plant you are diagnosing, using the dropdown menu. Then, you pick an image of the leaf of a plant and click diagnose. We will tell you what disease you have, give you example images, and tell you how to treat it.

IMPORTANT: Our AI model is not perfect, so make sure the description and example images match your issues. Additionally, info and treatments are not guarenteed to be accurate, this tool is designed to be used as a starting point for curing your plant.
